## What changed

- `SAIN/SAIN.csproj`: `DeployToGameDir` target copies `SAIN.dll` and `SAIN.Preset.Shared.dll` to `BepInEx\plugins\SAIN\`, keeping `.orig` (first ever) and `.bak` (previous). Game folder comes from `SAIN/SAIN.csproj.user` (not committed). Opt out with `-p:DeployToGame=false`.

- `DoorOpener.cs`: door cooldown timestamps are kept in `_allDoors` (they were lost on every refresh); bots only close open doors if the new `Bots Close Doors` setting is on (default off).
- `DoorOpener.cs` / `BotPathData.cs`: when a door is pulled open, the bot walks to a point outside the door's swing circle instead of the previous path corner; one path recalculation after each door interaction.
- `BotPathData.cs`: door-interaction movement passed a world position to `SetTargetMoveDirection` where a direction is expected; now passes `target - bot position`.

- `DoorOpener.cs` / `BotPathData.cs`: a bot that has made no progress for 1 s and is within 2.5 m of an open door closes that door (stuck-only last resort, independent of `Bots Close Doors`).

- `ForkSettings.cs` (F6: Global Settings > Fork Tweaks, saved in the preset): all fork-only settings. Reload: random suppress cutoff per magazine (`SAINBotSuppressClass.cs`), bots relocate to different cover when they start a reload in close quarters (`SAINCoverClass.cs`, `SelfActionDecisionClass.cs`). Doors: `Bots Close Doors` moved here from `DoorSettings`, plus switches and values for the stuck-close, swing retreat and interaction time. The SAIN server mod must run the matching `SAIN.Preset.Shared.dll`, otherwise it drops these keys when it saves the preset.

- Range behavior (branch `shot-reaction`, settings in Fork Tweaks, each with an off switch):
  - `WeaponInfo.cs` / `BotWeaponInfoClass.cs`: a bot's effective reach is the weapon-class reach times a factor for its sights (iron < red dot < optic, rising with the optic's zoom read from the item's `SightComponent`). Before, optics did not matter: an iron-sight AK scav counted as a 150 m weapon.
  - `SAINShootData.cs` (`AimAndShootAtEnemy`) and `EnemyDecisionClass.cs` (`shallShootDistantEnemy`): a bot does not aim or fire at a visible enemy farther than reach times `Shoot Reach Factor`. The cover and search layers shoot through `ShootAnyVisibleEnemies`, which had no distance limit.
  - `SAINBotSuppressClass.cs` (`TrySuppressEnemy`): no suppressing bursts at a last known position farther than reach times `Suppress Reach Fraction`. Before, there was no distance limit (rogues full-auto suppressing at 300 m).
  - `ForkBrainFilter.cs` / `BotProfile.cs`: per-brain toggles (PMCs, Rogues, Raiders, Scavs, ...). Range and Shot Reaction each have their own set (`RangeBrain*`, `ShotBrain*`, `BotProfile.RangeEnabled` / `ShotReactionEnabled`). A bot type that is off keeps the original behavior for that feature. Default on: PMCs and Rogues.

- Shot reaction (branch `shot-reaction`, Fork Tweaks "Shot Reaction", master switch plus the same brain filter):
  - `ShotReactionClass.cs` (new, on `BotComponent.ShotReaction`): a bot hit by, or passed closely by a bullet of, an enemy it cannot see gets a vague area (`DegradePosition`: random point 4-80 m around the shooter, error grows with distance, never exact) and one weighted roll, fight or flight: Cover (sprint to cover away from the shot, or flee 25 m with no cover points) or Fight (a search for the shooter, see below). Weights come from distance zones (Short/Mid/Long as fractions of the sight-adjusted reach, blended over a 60 m reference reach), pulled by weapon (sight factor, class: SMG/shotgun/full auto fight, marksman/sniper/semi-only cover), personality, shooter concealment (suppressor, prone), hit vs bullet pass, bot state (running, injured), and whether the bot faces the shot. Point blank (10 m or less) is a hard stop: Fight at least 80%, no concealment. Event driven, nothing ticks. A shot is "seen" if the shooter is visible within 0.4x reach at once, or for 1 s within 1.25x reach, held 4 s.
  - After a Cover hide with the shooter still unseen, a follow-up pick: Fight On (hold cover and fire, weights 35), Investigate (40: cover-to-cover hops toward the vague area, hold 3-7 s, sprint, stops at 15 m, on hearing or seeing, 90 s) or Retreat (25: relocate 40-80 m by chance-based routes and scored destinations, then hold cover 30-60 s and investigate). A Fight that runs out unseen continues as an investigation; an unseen hunt ends in a 60 s sprinting search. No re-roll while a state is active; a hit while investigating breaks it into Cover (15 s lockout). Self preservation: hit 90% / bullet 20% while in cover moves the bot to another cover, never during Fight or at point blank. A safety level drives the post-retreat hold and a retreat restart when shot again. Foliage is concealment, not cover: a 30 m path penalty in `FindCoverPoint` (fades with distance), a bot shot in a bush relocates to solid cover; with only foliage around it counts as no cover (fight or flight, never a walk).
  - Fight is a real fight (`ShotSearchAction.cs`, new `ECombatDecision.ShotSearch`, `CombatSoloLayer.cs`): a Fight roll against a shooter the bot has not seen used to hand the bot to SAIN's combat, which has little to do with an unseen enemy (it walked around until it saw the shooter, firing only if its suppression gates happened to pass). Now the bot faces the vague area, fires at it for 2.5 s (SAIN's `SuppressPosition`, aim 1.2 m above the area, only within 1.25 x weapon reach, ammo and reload respected) and uses SAIN's dogfight movement toward it for 4 s; seeing the shooter ends it at once. Settings `Fight: Search And Fire / Search Time / Move While Searching / Fire Time` and `Fight On: Fire Pause`. `[ShotReaction]` lines `fight: searching ...` and `blind fire (...)` show it.
  - Interrupts and close range (`ShotReactionClass.cs`, `EnemyGainSightClass.cs`): a shooter within 5 m, or 3 hits in 5 s while the bot hides, retreats or hunts, interrupts it into Fight; point blank (10 m) rolls Fight 95%; a managed bot spots a shooter within 5 m at 0.15 x the normal delay.
  - Investigation without closer cover: the bot may run toward the vague area on foot for up to 12 s (rolled per stall, 60%) instead of holding.
  - Squad (`ShotReactionClass.cs`, `BotDecisionManager.cs`): the squad decision (follow the leader) yields while a reaction manages the bot; retreat destinations get a bonus near squadmates; squad roles (mates fighting raise Investigate, mates hunting raise Fight On); squad alert (a bot hit by an unseen shooter lets mates within 40 m react as to a close bullet). Each has its own switch.
  - Grenades from cover (`ShotReactionClass.cs`, uses SAIN's `GrenadeThrowDecider` and `ThrowGrenadeAction`): after a Cover hide, before the follow-up, and at the investigation ambush, with per-follow-up chances, distance limits and a cooldown. Switchable.
  - Log tag: every `[ShotReaction]` line is labelled with the bot's spawn type and, for PMCs, its nickname.
  - `HearingSensor.cs`, `BotHitByEnemyClass.cs`: the hooks (position degraded for close bullets and heard gunfire; roll on hit or close bullet).
  - `EnemyDecisionClass.cs`: while the reaction says hide, the decision is `SeekCover`; otherwise the normal decisions run.
  - `EnemyKnownChecker.cs`: the forced forgetting of an unseen shooter was dropped; SAIN's own enemy memory decides.
  - `SAINBotSuppressClass.cs`: suppressing bursts only in the Fight On state while a reaction is active. Fight On also fires its own bursts at the vague area on a fire / pause cycle (`ShotReactionClass.TryFightOnFire`, hooked in `SeekCoverAction`), because SAIN's own suppression stays silent without a position it can trace.
  - `EnemyGainSightClass.cs`, `EnemyVisionDistanceClass.cs`: the bonus for spotting a shooter that fires at you (spot time x0.5, vision distance x1.5) is configurable and off by default for bots with shot reaction on.
  - Relocate (`ShotReactionClass.cs`, new `RelocateAction.cs`, new `ECombatDecision.Relocate`, `CombatSoloLayer.cs`): the retreat move to a navmesh point 40-80 m away on the far side from the vague shot area, then the bot resumes from there instead of returning to where it was shot at. Settings `Relocate ...` in Fork Tweaks; no point found or the bot sees the shooter = original flow.
  - `SAINCoverClass.cs`, `SeekCoverAction.cs`, `SearchAction.cs`, `Enemy.cs`, `SAINShootData.cs`, `BotWeaponInfoClass.cs`: cover search with the foliage penalty and scored retreat destinations, search at a run after an unseen hunt, shot-reaction state on the enemy, reach-blend and weapon-type helpers.
  - `ForkSettings.cs`: the preset settings for the shot reaction (category `Shot Reaction/...`: zones, point blank, no cover, bushes, routes, safety, repeat hits, timings), merged into the `No Bullshit` preset; the ledger of every value is kept outside the repo. `[ShotReaction]` log lines with the roll, look angle and a `trace:` after the reaction help tuning.
  - Over-reach fire (`BotWeaponInfoClass.IsInOverReachWindow`, used by `IsWithinShootReach` and the `outOfRange` check in `EnemyDecisionClass.shallStandAndShoot`): a bot that sees an enemy beyond its reach times Shoot Reach Factor still gets a chance (default 25%, falling linearly to 0 at 2.5 x reach) rolled once per 6 s that opens a ~3 s window in which it may aim and fire, instead of a hard cut-off. Direct fire only; suppressing keeps its 0.8 x reach cap.

- `References\` is no longer tracked in this fork (git-ignored). It holds game and third-party assemblies that upstream bundles; this fork does not redistribute them. To build, copy `References\` from the upstream repo at `v4.5.1` (`git show upstream/master:References/...` or a checkout of the tag) into the repo root; the files are used for compilation only.

- Combat cover, bushes (Fork Tweaks "Combat Cover"): `SAINCoverClass.cs` removes bush cover points from the candidates (`filterBushes`) when the season has no foliage (`Seasons.Controller`, per-season switches), once the player has met the bot (seen it or shot at or near it within a memory time), or when the bush is in the player's view. A bot that holds a bush when a banned season applies drops it, `RelocateFromFire` and `ShotReactionClass.DestinationCoverQuality` skip banned bushes, and a bot in a bush at close range no longer holds and fires back when shot at (`CombatBushLeaveClose`). Log lines `[CombatCover]`.
- Stalled retreat watchdog (Fork Tweaks "Combat Cover" > "Stalled retreat"): `ShotReactionClass.cs` (`IsRetreatStalled`, used by `ContinueRelocating`) ends a retreat in which the bot moved less than 2 m in 6 s and is not at its target; the bot then takes the nearest cover and holds it as after an arrival. Log line `relocation over: stalled (...)`.
- Duel clock in combat (Fork Tweaks "Duel" > "Clock"): `ShotReactionClass.cs` (`TryDuelRetreat`) also ticks the duel clock for a bot that was hit or shot close at while it has visuals on the player, and, faster, for a bot that sees the player but cannot reach him and is not firing; only while the shot reaction is not managing the bot. A bot that does not know the player's position can only leave, never push.
- Fork Tweaks menu (F6): settings carry `[Category("Section/Subsection")]` (Reload, Doors, Range, Shot Reaction, each with subsections including its master switches and brain filter). `AttributesGUI.cs` (`DisplayOptionsByCategory`, `DisplaySectionMenu`) draws such categories as two-level collapsible menus, collapsed by default and forced open while searching; categories without a slash draw as before. Menu labels of the reload relocate and shot relocate settings were prefixed to tell them apart.
- Rough areas and position error (`ShotReactionClass.cs`, `HearingSensor.cs`, `BotHitByEnemyClass.cs`; settings in Fork Tweaks "Shot Reaction"): every guessed position of an unseen player is a ring (inner fraction 0.4), never the exact point. The error follows the situation, not raw vision: base = max(the bot's sight error, distance x 0.10), x 0.85 for a hit, x audibility (distance over the weapon's audible range with suppressor, flat to 0.7, x4 at 1.3, up to x6), x environment (shooter inside a building and bot outside: x1 to 60 m rising to x2.5 at 200 m; outside to inside x1.5 at 250 m; building to building ignored), x concealment (prone, crouch, bush, gear), x tracer ramp; soft cap 50 m creeping to 75 m, hard ceiling for the rest. Sustained tracer fire from one spot ramps the error down per shooter (first x0.95 to x0.3 over 5 noticed rounds, decays one step per 60 s, 180 s memory) and only while the shooter is inside the rough area the bot holds. Each alert recipient uses its own distance and sight. A shared knowledge counter narrows every rough area per new piece of information (cooldown, floor, tracer pieces weigh up to double) and is shared with neighbours of other groups by weighted total. Log lines `position error ...` and `more information ...` show the factors.
- Rogue radio and mounted guns (`ShotReactionClass.cs`, `Patches/StationaryPatches.cs`, new): the vanilla Rogue communication is replaced for player targets (vs AI it stays vanilla). A hit, close bullet or witnessed kill of a Rogue raises a global alert after a reaction delay plus a distance delay; reports to the group are one rough, delayed report per second; an unseen kill gives a delayed man-down call at the corpse. Zone-entry exact positions and forced visibility and the map-wide hit broadcast are removed (`RogueNoZoneMagic`). Gunners on mounted guns leave the post (300 s, via a gate on the vanilla `StationaryWS` layer) when the shot is outside the gun's arc, when unseated, when too unsure where the shot came from (uncertainty above `MountedMgMaxUncertainty`), or after an answer grace without a reply; inside the arc they stay and answer. Mounted fire uses known positions only; area suppression bursts at the rough area; grenade launcher scatter past 100 m with start delay, short bursts and a range cap. A kill alert from the killer alone also reaches other squads within 40 m.
- Aim target (`EnemyAimTarget.cs`, `BodyPartToShootPatch.cs`, `AimDataPatches.cs`): a head-only peek gets aim scatter (`HeadOnlyScatterRadius`) instead of a torso redirect, and SAIN's picker always decides. New `[AimLog]` line for bots aiming at the player.
- Duel (Fork Tweaks "Duel"; `ShotReactionClass.cs`; model reference `docs/sain-duel-mechanics.md` outside the repo): a bot that has the player in sight and fires at him without hitting runs a duel clock (also in combat and for bots that cannot reach the player) with missed bursts in a window, resolve (health, squad, reach vs distance, equipment, personality, squad losses), and an outcome of keep fighting, push cover to cover toward the player, or break off (hidden retreat; close range retreats must end out of the player's sight; hurt retreat to heal with a chance to push anyway). Rifle class weights on the pull (marksman, sniper), exposure rolls for marksman and sniper rifles, longer forgotten-after time for slow rifles, squad loss and kill break-offs, repeat escalation, scaled retreat distances with fallbacks. Pressure retreat for Rogues counts only local pressure. A watchdog ends retreats that make no progress.
- Combat cover and perf: `SAINCoverClass.cs`, `CoverFinderComponent.cs`, `SainBotCoverData.cs` (cover finder uses the rough area as threat position, crouch behind low cover, spread bots over cover points, fewer searches), `PoseClass.cs`; `ShotPerf.cs` (new) times the per-shot code for the log, including the parts of report delivery; report delivery runs within a per-frame time budget (`ReportFrameBudgetMs`).
- Moved out: the Tagilla feel patch (AI PMCs count as danger) and the BTR hostility fix were built here and now live in the owner's CustomRpTweaks.Client; they are no longer in this fork.
- Report gate (`SAINBotLookClass.cs`, `EnemyPartsClass.cs`): a bot's look tick reports the player to its group only while SAIN's own sight check (real raycasts to the player's colliders) passed within the last 1 s; otherwise the report is dropped and the game's visible flag is reset. Fixes a raid where the game's flag stayed true without sight and one bot fed its squad the player's position about 18 times a second for 15 minutes. Log `[ReportGate]` when drops last over 3 s (2026-11-21).
- Visual error and radio blur (`ShotReactionClass.cs`, settings in Fork Tweaks "Sharing"): a sighting report uses its own error (sight ratio x concealment, for the bot that saw the player), not the shot model's audibility, environment or tracer. Shared information (reports, squad and Rogue alerts) is taken as the source sent it and blurred only by the listener's distance from the source: 2 m base, free to 20 m, +0.05 m per meter to 100 m, +0.2 m per meter beyond, soft capped; nothing is re-guessed from the listener's own distance. Log `shared sighting|alert from ...` (2026-11-21).
- Gunner sighting multiplier (`ShotReactionClass.cs`, Fork Tweaks "Gunners: suppression"): a gunner on a mounted gun judges a shared sighting with `MountedSightingErrorMultiplier` (2) times its error for the uncertainty limits, evaluated when it decides (2026-11-21).
- Not built: leg or crippling damage behaviour (injured state is already part of the fight or flight weights), duel exposure gauge.
